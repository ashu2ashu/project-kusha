# Methodological Limitations & Model Boundaries

Project KUSHA is a computer-assisted equity research screening aid. The analytical conclusions it generates are subject to explicit structural, statistical, and operational boundaries:

### 1. Non-Reproducible AI Pillar Scoring
Pillar scores are generated by Google Gemini large language models. Identical financial statements and prompts evaluated under the same model version across different runs may produce varying subscores. While post-processing arithmetic (banding, penalty deductions, and PIO score caps) is deterministic in Python, the underlying pillar scores are probabilistic AI extractions.

### 2. Forensic Quality Index (FQI) Coverage Threshold
The Composite FQI metric requires a minimum 75% evaluated check coverage (6 of 8 checks). Check FOR_08 (auditor tenure and qualifications) lacks an automated statutory feed and normally returns `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`. Consequently, 7 of 8 checks is the operational ceiling for complete evaluation.

### 3. Heuristic Nature of Forensic Tests
Forensic indicators are mathematical threshold checks (e.g., DSO expansion, CFO/PAT divergence), not judicial or regulatory fraud determinations. Passing all indicators indicates the absence of heuristic anomalies, not an absence of fraudulent activity or accounting misstatement.

### 4. Static Peer Reference Dataset
Peer benchmarking uses a static FY24 baseline comparison dataset. It does not reflect live industry developments, real-time index rebalancings, or unadjusted corporate restructuring. Cross-period comparisons trigger warning indicators and are not dynamically ranked.

### 5. Delayed and Unaudited Market Data
Price telemetry, valuation multiples, and historical statement lines are fetched from Yahoo Finance. Financial statements cover approximately 4 reporting years, are subject to provider restatement, and are unaudited by Project KUSHA.

### 6. Absence of Statutory Compliance Registries
The system does not interface with MCA21, CARO audit report databases, related-party disclosure registries, insider-trading disclosures (SEBI PIT), or judicial litigation repositories.

### 7. Exchange Archive Retrieval Dependency
Annual report PDF discovery relies on BSE public archive endpoints (`bseindia.com`). Ingestion may fail if filing naming conventions change, or may return an earlier filing period if an amended annual report is delayed.

### 8. Ephemeral Cache Age
Local evaluation cache entries replay previous analysis runs for up to 24 hours without querying the Gemini API. The UI displays the exact timestamp and age of the cached analysis.

### 9. Synthetic Demonstration Mode
Demonstration analyses (e.g., Tata Consumer sample) run against pre-computed synthetic fixtures. They do not represent live corporate filings or current fundamental research.

### 10. Valuation Compass Is Not a DCF
Fair value indications and price anchors in the Valuation Compass are model opinions derived from qualitative heuristic ranges. They do not constitute Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) calculations and contain no Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) or terminal value modeling.

### 11. Currency Conventions and Field Normalization
Financial metrics are reported in ₹ Crore (INR) unless explicitly designated otherwise. Balance sheet and cash flow classifications depend on third-party provider mapping conventions under Ind AS.

### 12. Not an Audit, Valuation, or Advisory Service
Project KUSHA is not a statutory audit, formal equity valuation, or SEBI-registered investment recommendation. It must not be used as the primary basis for investment decisions.
